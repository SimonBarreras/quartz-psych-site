## Evolutionary Arguments

- **Natural selection:**
  Members of a species who are better suited are more likely to breed and pass on their genes
	- **Sexual selection:**  
	  A fact of natural selection, sexual selection is how the best mate is chosen to produce and protect the most healthy offspring. 
	  you need to be noticed, and once you are noticed you need positive distinctiveness from others 
		  - **Intersexual selection:** 
		    Members of one sex (usually females; as they invest more) choose members of another sex based on certain characteristics. (showing off what you're capable off)
		    
		- **Intrasexual selection:** Competition within one sex for acces to mates. Usually the strongest/ fitterst gets to pass on their genes

- **Parental investment theory:** The sex that is psychologically required to invest more in offsrping, evolves to be more choosy regarding mates.
