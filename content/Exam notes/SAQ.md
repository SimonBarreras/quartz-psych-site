What makes an answer perfect? 

- The focus at the start: "One evolutionary argument is... and one study that looks at this is ..."
- A full explanation of the argument: ie. the theory behind it, the correct key vocabulary, etc
- Links: the theory & argument LINKED EXPLICITLY to specific facts from the study
- Understanding: you highlight details that show you know what's important

# Common problems

Neuroplasticity SAQ:
- **limited explanation** of neuroplasticity
- Study **isn't focused** on neuroplasticity but in localization of function
- Study is **not clearly described**
- No clear explanation on what the study teaches about the role of neuroplasticity

Social Identity Theory (SIT) SAQ:
- SIT is **not described in detail**
- Study is not relevant to SIT
- Response confuses SIT with Social cognitive theory
- No link described between the study and SIT
