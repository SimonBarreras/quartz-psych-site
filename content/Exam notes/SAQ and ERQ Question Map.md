> **FLAG - Buss (1989): You need this study for evolutionary explanation questions.** There is no substitute on your reviewed list. Buss is the only cross-cultural study of mate preferences and the only direct test of Parental Investment Theory in your vault. Without it, you cannot answer "Explain one evolutionary explanation of behaviour" or any ERQ on evolutionary psychology with a strong primary study. It is already in your vault and has been reviewed. The learning investment is low: aim, 10,000+ participants, 37 cultures, key findings (women value resources, men value youth/attractiveness), and the parental investment conclusion.
