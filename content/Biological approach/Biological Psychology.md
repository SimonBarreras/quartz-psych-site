Ways of studying the physical brain are: 
- Surgery / lessions / Accidents
- Electroencephalography (EEG)
- Autopsy
- Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)
- Functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI)
- X-Rays
- Positron emission tomography (PET)

___

The biological approach covers topics such as genetics and behaviour (e.g. twin studies), the brain and behaviour (e.g. neuroimaging research), and the role of [[Hormones In Human Behaviour]] and [[Neurotransmission]]. Key studies often cited include [[Caspi et al. (2003)]] (MAOA gene and aggression), [[Maguire (2000) - Taxi Driver Study]] (hippocampal volume), and [[Raine et al. (1997)]] (PET scans of murderers).
