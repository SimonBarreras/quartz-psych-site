#PSY [[Love and affection]]
____
* [[Karlson and Luscher (1959)]] found that as little as one molecule of butterfly pheromone bombykol released by a female butterfly could attract a male butterfly from over 2 km away.
* [[Bruce (1959)]] found that if a recently impregnated female mouse was placed in a cage with an unknown male, the chance of miscarriage increased dramatically to 41%.
* [[McClintock (1971)]] - Menstrual synchronicity
* [[Zhou et al (2014) - Boar AND & EST]] - Pheromones influencing identification of gender
* [[Doucet et al (2009)]] - Areolar  gland secretion and mother-child bond

• Primer pheromones that cause slow, long-term physiological changes, such as hormonal effects
• Signaling pheromones that produce rapid behavioural effects, such as mating.
