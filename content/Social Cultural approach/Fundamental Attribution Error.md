The fundamental attribution error (FAE) is ==the human tendency to overestimate the influence of a person's character or personality (dispositional factors) and underestimate the influence of situational factors when explaining that person's behavior, especially negative behavior==. For example, if someone cuts you off in traffic, you might think, "What a jerk!" (dispositional explanation), rather than considering they might be rushing to an emergency (situational explanation). This bias is prevalent in [individualistic cultures](https://www.google.com/search?safe=active&sca_esv=a81d3bc6d1dfba59&q=individualistic+cultures&sa=X&sqi=2&ved=2ahUKEwiL0_qx482PAxUvV6QEHXL8OiEQxccNegQINxAB&mstk=AUtExfCOsVgEvEYFbl-qEFJ2avOXP3WaxeQI17d4ArLCqAh0WU9aTqjlv80wcMT1wYlV6-Z5RixzKNj5wN3mylrnEKZ0uRKkAVQzywJVOA3VNbZaNi9THqG0qU2tGn5MjZBxNnQ&csui=3) and can lead to harsh judgments and misunderstandings.
