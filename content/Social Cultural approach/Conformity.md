#PSY [[Social Cultural]], [[Abrams et al (1990) - Asch paradigm]]
___
- Informational conformity
- Normative 
- Referential conformity
----

Normative conformity stems from a desire for social acceptance, avoiding punishment, or gaining approval, while informational conformity is driven by the belief that others possess correct information, especially in ambiguous situations. Referential conformity is a broader concept where individuals conform to the standards and expectations of a specific reference group they identify with and aspire to join, which can encompass both normative and informational aspects of that group.


____
>[!Note]
>Conformity refers to individuals changing their attitudes or behaviour as a result of real or imagined social pressures.
>Conformity is a result of social comparison and indirect social influence.

___
# **Compliance**

>[!Summary]
Refers to when individuals  change their behaviour publicly to fit in a group, while maintaining personal beliefs in private.

___
# **Identification**

>[!Summary]
>Refers to when individuals conform to a group behaviour due to the desire of being associated with them.

___
# **Internalization**

>[!Summary]
>Refers to when individuals adopt a group's beliefs/behaviour both publicly and privately because they align with personal values.
