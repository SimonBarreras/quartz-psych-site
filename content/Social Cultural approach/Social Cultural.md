The sociocultural approach covers social and cultural factors shaping behaviour, including [[Social Identity Theory]] , [[Cultural Dimensions]] (Hofstede), [[Conformity]] research, and the influence of [[Group Dynamics]] . Classic studies include [[Tajfel (1971) - Klee or Kandinsky]] minimal group paradigm, [[Sherif et al (1954) - Robbers cave]] study, and [[Bandura (1961), (1963) & (1965)]] Bobo doll experiment.

The Socio cultural approach in psychology is the study of peoples's behaviour in groups and how individuals relate to others. 

Behaviour studied include: Attitudes, Attraction, Aggression and Group Dynamics.
