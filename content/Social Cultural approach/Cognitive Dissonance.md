#PSY [[Social Identity Theory]]
___

>[!Note]
>If people behave in certain way that doesn't fit their beliefs, they will change their beliefs to fit their behaviour.
>
>Dissonance causes actual physical discomfort


Dissonance presents upon the presence of inconsistency between **Acts Vs Beliefs**

>[!Example]
>"I smoke cigarettes" VS "I am aware that smoking is unhealthy"

Humans feel a need for consistency and tense when inconsistency is present, hence, will follow an accumulative process to solve or reduce that inconsistency as it generates tension or uncomfortable feelings.

1. Change a thought
2. Change an aspect of behaviour
3. Add a thought (as a form of compensation)
4. Trivialize the inconsistency
