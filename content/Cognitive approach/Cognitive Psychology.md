The cognitive approach covers cognitive processing,[[Heuristics - Schema Theory]], Models of memory, cognitive, and emotion. Relevant studies include [[Bartlett (1932) - War of Ghosts]] study (reconstructive memory), [[Loftus & Palmer (1974) - Car collision]] (eyewitness testimony), and research on cognitive load or [[Dual Processing Model (Systems 1 &2)]]. 

**KEY Concept**

Resources needed for Thinking and decision making, memory and motor skill coordination can be remembered with the acronym A-E-S-R

A - Attention
E - Encoding
S - Storage
R - Retrieval

Different parts Information processing:

1. Attention
2. Perception (making sense of data)
3. Memory
4. Language (although this is not in the IB list)
5. Decision making

This works as a way to study 

- Recognition vs Recall
- Rehearsal: Maintaining vs elaborating
- Schemas
- Heuristics & "System 1"
- ***Reconstructive memory***

___

>[!Note]
>Memory is NOT measured by accuracy as it is not a library or a computer isolated from social influence and is NOT about the past.
>
>Memory is only efficient if there is forgetting and pruning.

Memory is: 
- Organic and active
- Reconstructive
- Transactive (shared) (Memory that relays on external sources)
- About the future: Processes are directed to future survival (used in future situations)

"*Memory is a collective rather than an individual process*"

**Memory involves:** 
- Semantic knowledge 
- Procedural Knowledge
