Bias: Systematic error in information processing, not using all information available, or not combining it rationally & logically

Reason: We are ***Cognitive Misers***, Save energy, evolved, heuristics, mental shortcuts, schemas of the world + scripts, substitute complex with simple.
And because we are **Social animals.**

Theory: Dual process model, system 1 & system 2, Traversky and Kahneman.
