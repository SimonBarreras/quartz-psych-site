The Dual Processing Model argues that there are two ways in which we make decisions:
	- **System 1 thinking**: Reliant on past information and schema to make quick and effortless decisions based on limited information.  
	- **System 2 thinking**: Much more effortful and requires conscious reasoning.

When we use System 1 thinking we tend to use [[Heuristics - Schema Theory]].  One example of a heuristic is “anchoring bias.”
