___
>[!Tip]
>Think of it as your "Inner Eye"

___

The Visuospatial Sketchpad is the Visual component of the STM. Work as a *temporary* store for visual and spatial information. 

Can receive information either from Sensory Memory or Long-Term Memory stores

___
Visual processing includes the storage and manipulation of patterns and movements in two or three dimensions, and it is crucial for localization and orientation as well as for coordination.

Holds information of **What, where and How**. What visual information is relevant, where in space is it and how is it located, shaped, placed or how to navigate it.

>[!NOTE]
>Is used for example, to find our way around our house and remember where we've left things and what those things look like.
