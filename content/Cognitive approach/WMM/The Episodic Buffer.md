[[Working Memory Model]]
___
>[!Tip]
>Think of it as a temporary hold for information, to maintain it accesible while another process takes place. Can be compared to the "hold" option when you are in a phone call and someone else calls.

The episodic buffer is the one that allows us to understand the world around us. While all information is being processed in separate STM stores, This Buffer, temporarily holds several sources of information active at once. While you, or your central executive decide what is needed and what requires your attention at the moment.

It can hold active auditory and Visual information as well as information / "memory traces"  or memory "outlines" from LTM, at the same time.

[[Baddeley & Hitch (1974)]] proposed the buffer as a temporary and passive display store until the information is needed, responsible for **Conscious awareness**. Additionally, argued that it has a limited capacity explaining why we cannot multi-task.
