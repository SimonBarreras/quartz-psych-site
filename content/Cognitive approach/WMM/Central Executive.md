The central executive works as an attention direction and control system, that is supervises and monitors operations of the sub-systems of processing and storage. Can be thought of as the one to decide how and when the sub-systems are used. 

The central executive is thought to have the capacity to focus attention, divide attention between two or more sources, and switch attention from one task to another.

The central executive has limited Capacity. Meaning you cannot attend to a lot of things at the same time. Additionally, it is also modality free. Therefore, it can process any sensory information, whether it be auditory or visual.

___

**[[Attention Control]]:**

Attention control was proposed by [[Baddeley & Hitch (1974)]], as the primary function of the central executive. 

Attention control happens in two different levels: 

- The **Automatic Level**: Control of habitual or routinary activities that relay on Schemas ([[Heuristics - Schema Theory]]). These are controlled more or less automatically or can be considered "sub-conscious", although not necessarily. **Requiere limited amount of attention**. (e.g. Cycling to school)

- The **Supervisory attention Level**: Controls planning and decision-making. Creates new strategies, schemas and "outlines" for the Automatic level when the old ones are no longer sufficient. It is also the one used for **Emergency situations** and situations that require **Self-regulation**. Is capable of comparing alternative courses of action and choosing the most beneficial one.

>[!NOTE]
>People are highly reliant on automatic processing for everyday things
