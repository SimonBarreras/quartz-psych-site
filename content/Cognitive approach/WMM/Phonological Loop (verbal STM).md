 [[Working Memory Model]]
___

>[!Tip]
>Think of it as your "Inner Voice" and "Inner Ear"


Phonological loop is the auditory component of the STM  and its divided into two sub-components.
- **The articulatory control system**: Inner voice. Holds information in verbal form. Articulatory loop is also believed to hold words ready for cognitive tasks (e.g. when preparing to speak).
- **The Phonological Store:** Inner ear. Holds auditory memory *traces*. Can receive direct information from sensory memory in the form of **auditory material**, from the LTM in the form of **Verbal Information**, and from the articulatory control system.

>[!NOTE]
>Research shows that a memory trace can only last from 1.5 to 2 seconds if it is not rehearsed by the articulatory control system.
