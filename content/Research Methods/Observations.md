#Qualitative [[Research Methods]]
___
Observations are a a **Quantitative Research method**, meaning it produces data in the form of detailed descriptions of behaviour.

>[!Note]
>Behaviour is observed in a natural or controlled setting without researcher interference nor manipulation of variables

"Observations capture first hand information in a naturally occurring situation"

There are six types of observations: 

1. Naturalistic: Subjects are observed in their natural environment
2. Controlled: Variables are purposefully controlled by researchers
3. Overt: Participants are aware of they are being observed
4. Covert: Participants are NOT aware of being observed
5. Participant: Researches integrate themselves in the group being observed
6. Non-participant: Researchers don't interact nor become part of the group

>[!Note]
>An observation can be more than one of these types at the same time
>
>e.g. Naturalistic covert non-participant observation
