#Quantitative [[Research Methods]]
___
>[!Definition]
>**Measurement of the extent pairs of related values of two variables tend to co-vary.** 

If one variable tends to increase with the other it is a **Positive correlation**. If one variable tends to decrease oppositely to the other its an **inverse** or **Negative correlation.**

A **curvilinear relationship** is a type of relationship where, when one variable increases, so does the other; up to a certain point, where it starts to decrease while the other continues to increase. Or when one increases and the other decreases; up to a certain point where it increases with the other one.
___
# Comparison

| Experimental Research                                                  | Correlational Research                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Used to test **cause-effect** relationships                            | Test **association**                                                         |
| IV is manipulated DV is measured                                       | Variables are observed with **no manipulation**                              |
| Extraneous variables are **controlled**                                | Limited control, so other variables may play a role                          |
| High internal validity allows to draw conclusions about **causality**. | High external validity allows to generalize to other populations or settings |
