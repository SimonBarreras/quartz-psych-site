___
**Case Studies**

>[!NOTE]
>**In-depth** study of individual, group or event to explore complex issues or phenomena in a real-life context

- Are a **Qualitative** research method.
	- Show **In-depth examination** (of a particular subject rather than a population)
	- Explore complex/rare psychological phenomena
	- **Detailed** observation, interviews, assessments (**Method triangulation**)
	- They are usually **NOT Generalizable** to larger populations
	- **Comprehensive** data about behaviour, experiences and context
	- **Longitudinal** (change observed over time)
