[[Research Methods]]
___
They all are tools for **collecting data** (often from large samples) through **structured** or **unstructured** formats. And can produce either #qualitative (open-ended) or #Quantitative (Close-ended) data.

- Flexibility
- Easy analysis
- Potential Bias
___
# Questionnaire

Any **written set of questions** to collect **qualitative** data on opinions or attitudes, sometimes referred as "written interviews"

___
# Surveys

Both, a **set of questions** and the process of collecting, aggregating and analyzing responses. **Quantitative** (e.g. Likert scales, 5 or 7 point are the most common2)
