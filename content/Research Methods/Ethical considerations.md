***"CARDUD"*** are the ethical norms/ consideration for research in psychology.

- Consent
- Anonymity
- Right to withdraw
- Deception 
- Undue stress and harm
- Debrief
___
Not following this **Norms** can suppose a practical issue of responsibility and a threat to  your and the institutions reputation. 
Your study is just one more in the field and history of psychology. It is important to protect the reputation of research, researchers and your institution from legal procedures & ensure future participants continue to want to take part.
